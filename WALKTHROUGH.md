# Foley of the Dead: walkthrough

The lift in the foyer has three sockets: a grille (VOICE), a brass hand (TOUCH), and an iron cage (HEART). Each floor gives one. A floor stays asleep until the floor below it is cleared. You can walk up the stairs at any time, but nothing upstairs reacts until the lift has its voice.

You carry one piece of evidence at a time. Devices go on your belt and don't take up your hand. Select a device with 1–5; press E to use it on whatever is under the crosshair.

## Ground floor: the VOICE

The Ribbed Hollow stands invisible at the head of the dining table. It only speaks when the house is silent, and only the thermal camera shows it. While it lives, it appears in the distance now and then, watches you, and fades.

1. **Foyer:** examine the lift. It says *I NEED THE VOICE OF THE DEAD.*
2. **Study (left of the foyer):** take the **voice recorder** from the cabinet. (Clue: the writing desk says *Papa only talks when the clock stops.*)
3. **Heat thread, in either order with step 5:**
   - **Parlor:** with the recorder selected (1), record the **[CRACKLING FIRE]** from the fireplace.
   - **Kitchen:** graft the fire onto the **frozen pantry** door. It thaws open.
   - Take the **thermal camera** from the pantry shelf.
4. **Dining room:** select the thermal camera (4) and examine the warm air at the head of the table. The Hollow appears and you mark it.
5. **Silence thread:**
   - **Dining room:** with the recorder, record the **[TICKING]** from the grandfather clock.
   - **Servants' hall** (behind the foyer, through the kitchen or the dining room): graft the ticking into the **coffin**. The house goes silent.
6. **Dining room:** with the recorder, record the Hollow's **[VOICE OF THE DEAD]**. It collapses.
7. **Foyer:** graft the voice into the lift's **speaker grille**. *Ground floor cleared.*

Optional: the **UV lamp** is on the kitchen counter. You need it upstairs. Under UV, handprints on the ground floor lead from the foyer to the Hollow.

## Upper floor: the TOUCH

Take the stairs up. The veiled woman, Mournveil, now drifts between upstairs rooms. She fades in, stays a few seconds, and moves on. Her dead husband lies on the bed in the master bedroom.

1. **Nursery:** take the **EMF detector** from the crib (optional; its bars rise as you get closer to her).
2. **Find Mournveil** while she is visible and record **[HER LULLABY]** with the recorder. (Clues: the rocking chair, and the wedding portrait in the library: *Only her song opens his hand.*)
3. **Master bedroom:** graft the lullaby into **the husband**. His fist opens. Take the **photo camera**.
4. **Steam thread, in either order with steps 2–3:**
   - **Bathroom:** record the **[RUNNING WATER]** from the bathtub. The steam starts to clear.
   - **Master bedroom:** graft the water into the dry **washbasin**. (Clue: the diary in the library.)
5. **Bathroom:** select the UV lamp (2) and examine the **mirror**. A handprint glows on the cold glass.
6. Select the photo camera (5) and photograph the mirror: **[HANDPRINT OF THE DEAD]**.
7. **Back down to the foyer:** graft the photo into the lift's **brass hand**. *Upper floor cleared.*

## Attic: the HEART

Ride the lift up (E on the lift door). The Carver stands with its arms across the round window, and its head follows you. It only looks away toward a voice.

1. Take the **[VOICE OF THE DEAD]** back out of the lift's grille. The lift goes dead, and you are stranded.
2. Graft the voice into the **scarecrow's** gramophone horn. The Carver turns toward it.
3. With the recorder, record the Carver's **[HEARTBEAT OF THE DEAD]**. It falls.
4. Graft the heartbeat into the lift's **iron cage**.
5. Take the voice back from the scarecrow and graft it into the **grille** again.

All three sockets are full. The lift takes you out of the house.

## Help

- **J** opens the journal of everything you've examined.
- The bottom-left list shows what you can hear and which direction it's coming from.
- **Z** undoes your last action. Press **R** twice to restart.
